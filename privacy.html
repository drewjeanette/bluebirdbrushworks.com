Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Blue Bird Brushworks ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you shop with us or request custom art, and how it's used.

Information We Collect

  • Order information: name, email, and shipping address, collected securely through Stripe Checkout when you place an order.
  • Custom art requests: name, email, and the details you share about your commission, submitted through our request form.
  • Cart contents: stored only in your browser's local storage so your cart persists between visits. This never leaves your device unless you check out.

How We Use It

  • To fulfill and ship your order
  • To email you about your order or custom request
  • To respond to questions you send us directly

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted providers to run our shop, and each only receives the information needed to do its job:

  • Stripe — processes payments and shipping details. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to how they handle your payment data. We never see or store your card number.
  • Formspree — delivers custom art request submissions to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare — hosts our website and processes orders.

Cookies

We use one small, essential cookie for our own admin login — it's not used to track visitors. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your Rights

You can ask us to review, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing us below.

Contact
